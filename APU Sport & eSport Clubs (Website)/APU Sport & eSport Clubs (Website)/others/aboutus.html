WE RECRUIT STUDENTS WITH EVENT EXPERIENCE

We support, advise, and govern all programmes related to extra-curricular activities in APIIT-APU Malaysia Award-Winning University. These activities vary from cultural to, educational to, intramural to, anything beyond your academic agenda!.

Being a university student can be one of your most exciting expeditions. Higher education opens up a world of new ideas. Intellectual, social, sporting, cultural, and also building lifelong friendships. Here at APU, we support you to take the time to explore not only the educational experience but also the wide range of social, sporting, and, cultural events in campus. It is our aim to ensure our students are graduated with a first-class experience and not just limited to an academic qualification.
