APU Sports Carnival

The month of July @ APU was an eventful one, particularly in encouraging the practice of a healthy living lifestyle. The APU Sports Carnival 2019 celebrated the importance of sports, health, teamwork and sportsmanship, through a record-breaking series of events that spanned over 1 month. The 2019 installment of the Sports Carnival was officially known as the largest Sports Carnival in our history, in which we recorded the highest amount of participation as well as the most variety of sports being played. In addition, this year’s Sports Carnival further encouraged the interaction of staff & students outside the classrooms and offices, as the Organizing Committee first introduced the Staff vs Students Friendly Matches for the Futsal, Netball & Volleyball events. The Staff teams won the Futsal and Volleyball events whereas the Student team championed the Netball match.

APU Men's Volleyball: APU vs LMU Men's Club Volleyball 2018 Highlights

APU Men's Club Volleyball was back at it again. On Sunday, December 2, 2018, APU had set up a scrimmage against LMU. This was the first time we actually had the entire team together. This was just another preseason match before the semester had ended.
Both teams had put on a great match. APU however had lost the match 3 sets to 1. Our first 2 sets, our momentum didn't really pick up, which led to us giving LMU a lot of free points. By the time the 3rd set was underway, our momentum had a shift but wasn't enough to push a game 5.
A big thanks to LMU for agreeing to scrimmage against us and traveling all the way down. Shout out to all our friends and families that came out and supported us. This was a learning experience for our team and hopefully next time, the next meeting will have better results. Anyways, enjoy the highlights!
