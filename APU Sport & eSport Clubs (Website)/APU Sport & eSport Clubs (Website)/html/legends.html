Mobile Legends Club

Welcome to Mobile Legends Club. Every week we organize tournament with money prize.The following are the rules and regulations of our Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Club's tournaments which applies to any teams who registered and invited teams. The following rules and regulations do not limit the level of competition and gameplay. Price for participation in the tournament is 25 RM from each team.
RULES AND REGULATIONS
1.Registration and participation
 • Teams can be consists of 5 members (compulsory) and 1 substitute (optional)
 • This tournament is open for APU/APIIT students
 • Each team must commit to the time that has been set beforehand by the organizers.
 • Team names/player names that are found to be offensive/vulgar will not be tolerated by the organizers and a request of name change is allowed during and before the competition, teams that are found not following this will be disqualified immediately.
2. Tournament Bracket
 • No team limit.
 • All matches will be played online.
 • The tournament is in single elimination mode.
 • Groups will be separated depends on how many teams.
 • Top 8 teams participating must have a distinguishing uncopyrighted team logo.
