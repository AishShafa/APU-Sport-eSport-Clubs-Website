FIFA Club


Welcome to FIFA club, in our club we are playing FIFA on consoles. Also, we organize two types of tournaments every weekend. There are different prizes for winners. Do not lose your chance to win prizes and show your skills in playing FIFA. Here you can find a lot of interesting players to play with and teammates.

First is a single-player tournament:
  ♦ Draw for players
 ♦ 10 min game
 ♦ Fee 5 RM for each tournament
 ♦ Avenue: APU 3rd level
 ♦ Saturday from 3 pm to 6 pm
 ♦ Strictly only for members

Second is the duo-player tournament:
  ♦ Draw for teams
 ♦ 12 min game
 ♦ Fee 5 RM for each tournament(each player)
 ♦ Avenue: APU 3rd level
 ♦ Sunday from 2 pm to 6 pm
 ♦ For members and non-members

Also, we try to improve APU students' communication skills and help them in daily studies with sharing experiences.
