Football Club




The APU football club, offers :

• Students the opportunity to combine high performance football with a top degree
• Students the chance to develop their skills using our school facilities
• Recreational players the platform to compete on a casual basis


A platform for talent to thrive
    The University’s football facilities are utilised by students which helps discover and develop talents. Using the same facilities, the football club aims to develop young players with excellent coaching and a thriving performance centre.

Accessible by everyone
    Alongside the University’s football club, there are even more opportunities for recreational players to get involved. The university’s set-up includes futsal teams, inter-halls leagues, inter-department teams and lower standard 11-a-side teams among much else.
